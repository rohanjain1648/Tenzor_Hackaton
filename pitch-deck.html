Risks

Risk Landscape & Mitigation

⚠️ Regulatory Risk

RBI VKYC guidelines are evolving. AI-generated underwriting may require human-in-the-loop mandates.

Mitigation: Policy engine is fully configurable. Human escalation mode built-in. RBI Sandbox application planned Q3 2026.

🔐 Data Privacy Risk

Video biometric data is highly sensitive under DPDP Act 2023 with cross-border restrictions.

Mitigation: AWS India data residency. AES-256 at rest. E2E encrypted WebRTC. Verbal consent captured in-session.

🤖 AI Hallucination Risk

LLM may misclassify income or risk band from ambiguous transcripts, causing incorrect loan decisions.

Mitigation: Strict JSON schema enforcement. Low-confidence sessions escalated to human review. Confidence scores in API output.

📶 Connectivity Risk

WebRTC quality degrades on poor rural networks; borrowers may drop mid-session.

Mitigation: Adaptive bitrate. TURN server fallback. Async audio-only mode for low-bandwidth environments.

💸 LLM Cost Risk

High Gemini API token costs at scale could compress margins on lower-tier plans.

Mitigation: Gemini Flash (lowest cost tier). Transcript chunking. Rule-based pre-filter before LLM call.

🏦 Sales Cycle Risk

Enterprise NBFC procurement is 6–18 months. Cash runway may be stressed before revenue.

Mitigation: Prioritize fintechs (2–4 month cycle). Seed covers 18 months. Pilot-to-paid path shortens cycle.
